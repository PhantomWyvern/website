About

Hi, I'm Michael.

michaelk-research.dev is my public study notebook as I work towards a career maintaining equipment in a nuclear power plant. I'll use it to record what I'm learning and explain it in my own words.

Nuclear science was the A-level Physics topic that stayed with me—even though I earned an E. I went into vehicle repair, where I realised I understand things best when I know why and how something works. That's the approach I'm bringing to nuclear engineering now.

I'm currently applying to university and apprenticeship routes to find a path into the industry.

The question I'm exploring

How does controlled fission become electricity, and why does a PWR need each part of the process? I want to follow the energy from the reactor core through the coolant loops and turbine, and understand the physics behind each step—not just memorise equations.

My roadmap

  1. A-level Mathematics: Year 1 fundamentals — In progress
  2. A-level Mathematics: Year 2 topics — Next
  3. A-level Physics review — heat transfer, moments, energy, 2D vectors and radioactive decay
  4. Further topics — I'll add these as I progress
